# Saffron & Table — design brief

An independent culinary brand for office hosts, friends gathering at home, and people planning a considered celebration. The concept is a contemporary kitchen journal: generous food photographs, finely ruled menus, a typographic ampersand, numbered service modes, and a receipt-like order summary.

Warm ivory #f8f5ee carries most of the interface; charcoal #292e29 anchors actions. Ochre #b97828 is used sparingly for rules and small graphic accents, with darker brown for readable accent text. Muted olive supports dietary information. Georgia provides restrained editorial headings; system sans-serif keeps forms, prices and navigation clear without external font requests.

Photography uses natural light, ceramic serving dishes and real food texture. Large landscape crops introduce the gathering; consistent product crops support comparison. Layout follows a bounded 1280px grid, 8px spacing increments, restrained borders, 4px controls, and almost no shadow. Mobile uses one content column, an accessible menu, and a bottom cart action. Motion is limited to short transitions and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Drop-off is a browse/add/check-delivery flow. Full-service is a three-step guided inquiry, with an itemized indicative estimate and explicit approval language. Prices and policies are fictional demo configuration, not real commercial offers. No payment or message is sent.

## Reference matrix

All five sites were read through actual live page content on 6 November 2026. Rendered desktop/mobile reference inspection was unavailable: the browser inventory is empty and the Windows Computer Use native pipe is unavailable.

| Website                                                              | Page inspected                   | Useful observed pattern                                                                                | Adaptation                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [ezCater](https://www.ezcater.com/)                                  | Homepage ordering entry          | Delivery address appears at the beginning of ordering; food categories are scannable                   | Delivery planning sits above the menu; categories remain compact                       |
| [CaterCow](https://www.catercow.com/cuisines/mediterranean-catering) | Mediterranean package listing    | Group-friendly package names, visible prices, and category navigation                                  | Price and serving size share the product footer; food and dietary filters are separate |
| [Ottolenghi](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/pages/catering)                | Catering overview and FAQs       | Online ordering and tailored event contact are distinct; notice and dietary requirements are explained | Two explicit service modes; clear timing and allergy notes close to decisions          |
| [Rhubarb](https://rhubarbhospitality.com/)                           | Hospitality homepage             | Event planning has its own primary action and occasion categories                                      | A separate event form organized around occasion, service and guest details             |
| [Dishoom](https://www.dishoom.com/)                                  | Homepage food/editorial sections | Food descriptions, image captions and menu links create a hospitality voice                            | Brief ingredient-led copy, serif hierarchy and small editorial captions                |

The design synthesizes these functional ideas. It does not reuse source code, branding or proprietary copy.
